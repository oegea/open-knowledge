# Contributing to Open Knowledge

Thank you for caring about this project. Open Knowledge is intentionally a
small, personal, opinionated tool — and keeping it that way is part of what
makes it trustworthy and easy to self-host.

## What we welcome

**Ideas, suggestions and bug reports — as issues.** If something is broken,
confusing, missing, or could simply be better, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/oegea/open-knowledge/issues). Real-world
reports from people running their own libraries are the most valuable input
this project can receive. Feature ideas are welcome too, especially when they
come with the *why* behind them.

## What we don't accept

**Code contributions (pull requests).** This is deliberate, not a lack of
appreciation: a single maintainer keeping every line coherent is how the
project stays simple, private by design, and auditable. Pull requests will be
closed, but the ideas inside them are still welcome as issues.

Since the project is MIT-licensed, you can always fork it and take your copy
anywhere you want — that freedom is the point.

## A note on scope

Open Knowledge follows a simplicity principle: no social features, no
analytics, no personal data, no plugin systems. Suggestions that fit that
spirit have the best chance of landing.
